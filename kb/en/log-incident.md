---
slug: log-incident
title: "How do I log a student incident or injury?"
screen: /os/incidents/new
module: incidents
---

1. Open Incidents and tap New.
2. Choose the type, students involved, place and time.
3. Describe what happened and the action taken; set severity and confidentiality.
4. Add follow-up tasks and save. Serious cases notify the principal at once.
