slug: apply-leave
title: "How do I apply for leave?"
screen: /os/leave/apply
module: leave
---

1. Open Leave and tap Apply.
2. Choose the leave type and the dates, or a half day.
3. Check your balance and who else in your department is on leave.
4. Add the reason, a document if needed, and a hand-over note.
5. Tap Submit. It goes to your approvers in order.
