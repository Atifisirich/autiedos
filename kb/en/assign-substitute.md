title: "How do I assign a substitute?"
screen: /os/substitution
module: substitution
roles: [academic_coordinator, vice_principal, principal]
---

1. Open Substitution for today.
2. Each affected period shows ranked free teachers.
3. Tap the best match to assign. The teacher gets an alert, a task and the cover plan.
4. If no one is free, choose combine, library, games or supervision.
