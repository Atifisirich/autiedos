title: "How do I send a circular?"
screen: /os/circulars/new
module: circulars
roles: [principal, admin_officer, vice_principal]
---

1. Open Circulars and tap New circular.
2. Write the title and message and attach files.
3. Choose who receives it: branch, wing, department, role or people.
4. Choose read receipt, acknowledge or reply, then send now or schedule.
