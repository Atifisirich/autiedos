1. Open Setup. The wizard has 8 steps.
2. Enter the school profile and board, then the academic year and bell schedule.
3. Download each Excel template, fill it and upload: structure, subjects, staff, org chart.
4. Check roles and switch on modules. Row errors are shown before anything is saved.
