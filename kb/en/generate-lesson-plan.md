title: "How do I create a lesson plan with AI?"
screen: /os/lesson-plans/new
module: lesson_planner
---

1. Open Lesson plans and tap New daily plan.
2. Choose section, subject, chapter and topic from the library.
3. Check the periods, teaching model and resources.
4. See the credit cost and tap Generate.
5. Read and edit the plan, then tap Confirm before submitting.
