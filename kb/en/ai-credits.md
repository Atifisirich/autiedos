title: "How do AI credits work?"
screen: /os/ai-credits
module: ai
---

1. Each branch gets monthly credits from its plan.
2. Every AI action shows its cost before it runs.
3. Warnings come at 80 and 95 percent; at 100 percent generation stops until a top-up.
4. Admins can request a top-up from AI credits.
