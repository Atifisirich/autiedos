slug: assign-task
title: "How do I assign a task?"
screen: /os/tasks/new
module: tasks
---

1. Open Tasks and tap New task.
2. Type the title and choose the category and priority.
3. Under Assign to, pick a person, or a role, wing or department.
4. Set the due date and whether photo proof or verification is needed.
5. Tap Save. The person gets a notification.
