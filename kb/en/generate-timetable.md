title: "How do I generate the timetable?"
screen: /os/timetable/generate
module: timetable
roles: [principal, vice_principal]
---

1. Check teacher allocations and bell schedules are complete.
2. Open Timetable and tap Generate.
3. Review unplaced periods and the suggested fixes.
4. Drag to adjust, lock cells, then Publish with an effective date.
