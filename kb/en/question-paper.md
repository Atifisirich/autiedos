---
slug: question-paper
title: "How do I create a question paper?"
screen: /os/exams/papers/new
module: exams
---

1. Open Exams, then Blueprints, and pick or create a blueprint.
2. Tap Generate paper, choose sets and source: bank, AI or mixed.
3. Replace any question, then Submit for review to your HOD.
4. Papers stay hidden from others until the exam date.
