## 2. Services

1. AutoEd grants the Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the products in the Order Form during the term, for the Client's own educational administration.
2. Only the products bought are enabled. Data and features of a product the Client has not bought are not accessible to it.
3. AutoEd may update the Services. Material reductions are notified in line with the Terms of Service.
4. Implementation, training and data migration are separate services if listed in the Order Form `[ASSUMPTION]`.

## 3. Roles under data protection law

1. For personal data in the Services about learners, parents/guardians, staff and enquirers (including CRM leads), the **Client is the Data Fiduciary** and **AutoEd is the Data Processor** (decisions #31, #48). The DPA in Annexure A governs.
2. For Client contact and billing data that AutoEd collects for its own purposes, AutoEd is a Data Fiduciary. See the Privacy Policy.
3. AutoEd's own marketing or prospect data is kept in a separate AutoEd-owned organisation and never mixed with the Client's data (decision #31).

## 4. Client responsibilities

The Client:

1. is solely responsible for the **lawfulness of the data it enters**, for giving notices to data principals, and for obtaining and recording consent, **including verifiable parental/guardian consent for children's data**;
2. will not require AutoEd to track, profile or run targeted advertising directed at children;
3. will keep its users' access current, protect credentials and promptly report suspected misuse;
4. will obtain and keep **opt-in consent** for WhatsApp and any SMS messages it sends through the Services, and will comply with Meta/WhatsApp policy, TRAI/DLT rules and anti-spam law (`messaging-consent.md`);
5. will not put student or staff personal data into AI prompts where the product warns against it;
6. will respond to data-principal requests (access, correction, erasure, grievances) and will use the tools in the Services to do so (`data-subject-rights-procedure.md`);
7. will, if it brings its own WhatsApp provider, hold the right to that account and meet that provider's terms.

## 5. AutoEd responsibilities

AutoEd will:

1. provide the Services with reasonable skill and care;
2. process Client personal data only on documented instructions and as set out in the DPA;
3. keep Client data **in Mumbai, India** for the database and file storage, and disclose the sub-processors and locations in the DPA;
4. enforce tenant and product isolation in the database and test it before release;
5. never send student or staff personal data to AI providers and use Google Gemini only on the paid tier;
6. log AutoEd staff access to the Client's account (time-limited, with reason, visible to the Client);
7. notify the Client of a personal data breach as set out in the DPA `[timeline CONFIRM]`;
8. export and delete data as set out in section 9.

## 6. Fees and payment

1. Fees and GST are as in the Order Form and are payable via Razorpay (or by invoice if agreed).
2. Renewal, price changes, refunds and cancellation follow `refund-and-cancellation-policy.md`.
3. Late payment: product suspension to read-only per that policy; interest at `[CONFIRM rate, e.g. 1.5% per month]` on overdue invoices `[CONFIRM enforceability]`.
4. If the Client disputes an invoice in good faith it pays the undisputed part and notifies AutoEd within `[15 days - CONFIRM]`.

## 7. Term and termination

1. The agreement runs for the initial term in the Order Form and renews for successive terms of the same length unless cancelled before renewal.
2. Either party may terminate for the other's material breach if not cured within `[30 days - CONFIRM]` of written notice.
3. AutoEd may suspend on `[7 days' - CONFIRM]` notice for non-payment and immediately for security risk or unlawful use.
4. On termination, section 9 applies.

## 8. Intellectual property and data ownership

1. AutoEd owns the Services and related IP.
2. The Client owns all Client data, including CRM leads. AutoEd gets a licence to host and process it solely to provide the Services.
3. AutoEd may use **aggregated, de-identified** usage statistics to run and improve the Services, never in a form that identifies a Client, person or child `[ASSUMPTION; counsel to confirm acceptability under the DPDP Act]`.

## 9. Data on non-payment and on leaving

1. **Non-payment of one product:** that product becomes read-only for **30 days**, then the Client's data in that product is exported and deleted (decision #42; details in the Refund and Cancellation Policy).
2. **Leaving entirely:** AutoEd provides a **CSV/Excel export** and deletes the Client's data **30 days later** (decision #42).
3. The Client is responsible for downloading its export. After deletion, AutoEd cannot recover it.
4. AutoEd keeps its own financial records (invoices, payments) as the law requires `[CONFIRM ~8 years]`.
5. Written confirmation of deletion is available on request.

## 10. Confidentiality

Each party protects the other's confidential information using at least reasonable care and uses it only for this agreement. Exceptions: public information, independently developed, or disclosure required by law.

## 11. Warranties, liability and indemnity

1. AutoEd warrants it has the right to provide the Services. Other warranties are disclaimed to the extent the law allows.
2. Nothing excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.
3. Cap on each party's total liability in a 12-month period: fees paid in that period `[CONFIRM]`, **except** for `[CONFIRM: breach of confidentiality, indemnities, AutoEd's breach of the DPA - possibly a super-cap]`.
4. No liability for indirect or consequential loss `[CONFIRM]`.
5. The Client indemnifies AutoEd for claims arising from the Client's lack of lawful basis or consent for its data, or its breach of section 4. AutoEd indemnifies the Client for third-party IP claims about the Services `[CONFIRM both]`.
6. Statutory penalties under the DPDP Act fall on the party whose breach causes them; allocation between AutoEd and Client for a breach caused by AutoEd is a negotiated point `[CONFIRM]`.
